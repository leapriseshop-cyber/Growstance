The Challenge:

The Transformation:

Hospitality & Facilities

TrueCare Hotels

The Challenge:

A hospitality powerhouse managing 25+ hotels, 500+ projects, and greentech solutions — but their digital presence failed to communicate scale, credibility, and service diversity.

The Transformation:

We built a multi-vertical platform showcasing hotels, events, management, and greentech in one cohesive experience — with trust stats, hotel portfolio carousel, and clear service segmentation.

Result: A unified digital identity serving 4 business verticals, driving direct bookings, and establishing pan-India authority.

Solar Energy & Renewable Power

The Challenge:

An IIT-founded solar energy company with 1000+ projects and 25+ years of expertise — trusted by Indian Railways — yet their website didn't reflect the engineering authority of their operations.

The Transformation:

We crafted a commanding dark-gold digital presence with structured service pillars (EPC, Products, O&M), geo-tagged project highlights, and prominent trust signals that establish solar authority.

Result: An authoritative platform communicating 15MW+ capacity, real project installations, and converting high-value B2B solar inquiries.
